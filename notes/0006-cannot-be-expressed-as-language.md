There is knowledge that cannot be expressed as language. This page is a text attempt at that corpus, which is impossible: text is the one modality the corpus is about what escapes. The only move is to use language to point at where language gives out, and to be honest that the pointing is not the thing.

1. Love.
2. Emotions more generally. We can express "anger" through metaphors like "seeing red", but the language is incomparable to the emotion.
3. Music. It's quite difficult to express the difference between music and noise, but we know it when we hear it.
