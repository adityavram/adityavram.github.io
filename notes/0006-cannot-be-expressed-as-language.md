---
title: In Progress - The corpus of knowledge that cannot be expressed as language
date: 2026-11-22
tags: [knowledge, language, ineffable, in-progress]
status: public
---

1. Love.

2. Emotions more generally. We can express "anger" through metaphors like "seeing red", but the language is incomparable to the emotion.

3. Music. It's quite difficult to express the difference between music and noise, but we know it when we hear it.

4. Good writing. There is a difference between good writing and bad writing. But the set of rules is very rarely well defined.

5. Work ethic. Some people do work harder than others, yet that bar is very difficult to put into words.
